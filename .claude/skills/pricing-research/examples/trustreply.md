# Example: Trustreply
Source: `ventures/trustreply/05-strategy.md` (pricing hypothesis, all assumptions).
- Value anchor: 8-20 hours per questionnaire x $75-150/hour founder time = roughly $600-3,000 per questionnaire (unverified; confirm in interviews).
- Alternatives: manual spreadsheet (time cost), compliance suites (often priced far higher), specialist questionnaire tools (demo-gated).
- Customers needed for ~$13k MRR: ~60 at a blended ~$215.
- Tiers: Starter $149 (5 questionnaires, 1 user); Team $349 (unlimited, 5 users, library, audit export).
- Test: pre-sell Starter at $149 and $99 to two comparable audiences; compare paid conversions.
