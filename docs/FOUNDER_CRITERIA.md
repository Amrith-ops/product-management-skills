# Founder criteria (as given)
Source: founder message, 2026-10-24. Wording kept close to the original; light edits for clarity.
**Status: flexible guides for choosing a domain, not hard gates.** Adjust to what works for a solo founder.

Context: solo founder in India, keeps a 9-5 job, targets Western customers.

1. **Earn in dollars, not rupees.**
2. **Few clients, high revenue.** Reach USD 16-20k/month with about 30-50 clients, so customer support fits alongside the 9-5 job. Target ultra-rich domains or family businesses, because their sales cycle is short and they can be sold quickly, without months of compliance and bureaucracy.
3. **No field work.** Operate from India while serving Western customers.
4. **Reachable as a group.** Clients can be reached in social communities as a group, for marketing and sales.
5. **Less tech-savvy buyers.** They cannot code a fix themselves, or are too busy to.
6. **High profit margin** ("> 100%"; read as very high margin or return, since gross margin cannot exceed 100%).
7. **Under the radar, but the pain is very high.** Competition can be tackled or managed.
8. **Hair on fire.** The problem is urgent.
9. **Room in existing software.** Find out how much software already occupies the domain: is there a pie to eat?
10. **Operating cost under INR 50k per month.**
11. **No legal battles or compliance penalties.**
12. **Buildable by a solo entrepreneur.** Understand what can and cannot be done solo.
