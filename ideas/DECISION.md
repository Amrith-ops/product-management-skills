# Decision: what to work on
Date: 2026-10-24. Replaces open-ended research with one chosen bet, a 14-day test, and a fallback.

## Why it feels unresolved
- 39 ideas, 12 criteria, B2B vs B2C, and four research rounds: more input has not added more certainty.
- Desk research cannot prove demand. Only conversations and money can.
- Rule from here: **no new research until the sprint below ends.**

## Decision
**Work on A (Trustreply) as a 14-day concierge test, not a build.** It is the only candidate where pain, reachable buyers, price evidence and buildability are all backed by something I could find. Criteria are flexible, and the miss (technical buyers, not family-owned) is acceptable because technical founders decide fast and reach in public communities is proven.

**Fallback:** if A fails its gate, run the same sprint on B, but start by finding where distributors gather (community-discovery) before any outreach.

## 14-day sprint (about 10 hours a week alongside a 9-5)
| Days | Action | Output |
|---|---|---|
| 1-2 | Post one correction-style question in a relevant community; send 10-15 personal messages to people who described the pain (per `research-outreach`) | Replies and conversation volunteers |
| 3-9 | 8 short conversations: how many questionnaires, hours each, who answers, what happened to the deal, what they pay for compliance | Notes, hours and dollars stated |
| 6-12 | Offer 3 paid pilots: you fill one real questionnaire from their documents with AI help and citations, they review; price $199-$400 for the pilot | Accuracy rate, willingness to pay |
| 13-14 | Decide against the gate; write what you learned | Go, pivot or stop |

## Gate (pre-registered)
- **Go to MVP:** at least 3 of 8 report 3+ questionnaires in the last 6 months, and at least 2 pilots pay, and median acceptance of drafted answers is 70% or better with no answer presented as fact without a citation.
- **Pivot:** pain is real but nobody pays, or price tolerance is under $100 a month: change the offer or buyer, run again.
- **Stop and switch to B:** fewer than 2 of 8 have the pain, or pilots are refused on data-trust grounds with no workaround.

## Risks to watch
- Selling to people who can code the fix themselves (they may build a script). Ask directly.
- Data trust: buyers may refuse to share policies. Offer redaction and deletion after each pilot.
- Revenue goals ($12-15k a month) are targets, not forecasts.
