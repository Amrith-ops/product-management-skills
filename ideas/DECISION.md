# Decision: what to work on (v3, supersedes v2)
Date: 2026-10-24.

## What changed
- The founder rejected the janitorial distributor idea because the field is low margin: thin-margin customers limit price tolerance. **Jan-san and the distributor order desk are parked** (`ideas/distributor-order-desk.md`, `ideas/janitorial-vertical.md`).
- Checked and rejected since v1: Trustreply (technical buyers), importer tariff desk (15 of 24), horse leads (13), manufacturing quoting/quality (15-17), CMMC compliance (14).

## Why guessing ideas has not converged
The criteria pull against each other: non-technical buyers, high price per customer, high margin, low legal risk, solo-buildable, little competition, a data moat and urgency. Every field I checked met some and failed others. More idea-by-idea guessing will keep producing 13-17 scores.

## New approach: pick the customer first, let conversations pick the problem
**Rule for the customer:** owner-run businesses where (1) gross margins are healthy, (2) one new customer is worth $5,000 or more, (3) owners already spend $1,000+ a month on getting customers, (4) owners are not software-minded, and (5) reachable communities exist.
**Segment with evidence already collected:** high-ticket home-services owners (roofing, remodeling, solar, HVAC replacement, pools).
- Reach is verified: an active roofing community on Reddit (reported 80,000+ members), RoofingTalk, ContractorTalk, and Facebook groups such as Contractors Helping Contractors and Roofing Business Owners Only. ([JobNimbus forums](https://www.jobnimbus.com/blog/top-roofing-forums), [Facebook groups](https://jobnimbus.com/blog/the-7-best-roofing-groups-to-join-on-facebook); vendor blogs, check activity yourself.)
- Spending is verified: service contractors report about $2,000-$6,000 a month on marketing including ad spend, with cost per lead of $40-$150 (roofing about $150-$230). ([ClicksGeek](https://clicksgeek.com/?p=17330), [Enrich Labs](https://www.enrichlabs.ai/blog/roofing-lead-generation-complete-guide-2026); vendor claims.)
- Value per customer is high: single jobs often worth thousands. 🔶
**Higher-margin alternative to test later:** cosmetic and med-spa clinics (unverified; texting-consent and health-privacy risk).
**Known risks:** crowded with CRMs and AI receptionists; thin to moderate net margins for roofers; texting leads needs consent compliance (TCPA); you start with no domain knowledge or contacts.

## Sprint (14 days, about 10 hours a week)
| Days | Action | Pass |
|---|---|---|
| 1-4 | Join 3 communities as a learner (read rules; no pitching); read 50 recent posts about leads, follow-up, quotes and cash flow; list the 10 most repeated complaints | A ranked list of complaints with links |
| 3-9 | 8 conversations with owners, using one question: "What is your most expensive problem in getting and closing jobs?" | At least 5 agree to talk; 3 name a problem costing $2,000+ a month |
| 8-14 | Pick the one problem named most often; sell a manual version (concierge) to 2-3 owners for $300-$800 a month, or on a pay-per-result basis | At least 2 pay |

## Stop rules
- Fewer than 5 owners willing to talk after 20 asks: change segment (try cosmetic clinics).
- Nobody pays for a manual version: the problem is not painful enough; do not build software.
- No new idea research until the sprint ends.

## Before taking payment
Check the employment contract and Indian export-tax setup (a CA can confirm). If texting leads, get legal advice on consent rules.
