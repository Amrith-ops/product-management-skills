# Idea check: CMMC compliance documentation for small defense manufacturers
Date: 2026-10-24. From the assistant's knowledge, then verified once with web searches (vendor and law-firm pages; mostly marketing). 🔶 assumption; 🔵 open question.

## Verdict
**Not recommended. Score about 14 of 24.** Customers can afford it and the buyers are non-technical, but the deadline that made it urgent was paused, many vendors already compete, and legal exposure sits close to the product.

## Findings
| Question | Result |
|---|---|
| Is the deadline real? | Phase 2 (Level 2 third-party assessments) was scheduled for November 10, 2026, but the Department of War paused it on July 13, 2026 and set up a 60-day reform task force; Phase 1 self-assessments continue. I found no newer source on a new date. [DLA Piper](https://www.dlapiper.com/en-it/insights/publications/2026/08/department-of-war-suspends-cmmc-phase-2-assessment-requirements-top-points), [Secureframe](https://secureframe.com/blog/cmmc-phase-2-preparation) |
| Do customers spend? | Yes: DoD's own estimate for a small entity is about $37k (self-assessment cycle) to about $105k (third-party certification cycle); vendor estimates for readiness work run $3.5k-$45k and all-in $40k-$250k. [PreVeil](https://www.preveil.com/blog/cmmc-certification-costs/), [Petronella](https://petronellatech.com/blog/how-much-does-cmmc-certification-cost-in-2026-a-straight-answer-for-defense-contractors/) |
