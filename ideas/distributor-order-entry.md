# Parked idea: AI order entry for small family-owned distributors
Status: parked (not validated). Date: 2026-10-24. Full reasoning: `ventures/domain-selection/domain-scorecard.md`.

## One line
Customers forward emailed POs to a dedicated address; AI extracts the order, flags uncertain fields, the owner approves, and a clean spreadsheet/CSV (plus confirmation email) comes out. No ERP integration at first.

## Who
Owner-run wholesale distributors, roughly $5-50M revenue, starting with one vertical (e.g., foodservice, building supply, industrial or janitorial supplies).

## Why it is promising
- Orders arrive by email/PDF/spreadsheet/fax and are retyped into an ERP; one vendor claims ~20 min and $10-12 per order (unverified).
- Low-tech buyers, no field work, USD pricing, software margins, low legal risk.
- Target: 30-50 clients x $400-650/mo = $16-20k/mo.

## Criteria are guides, not gates
The 12 founder criteria (`docs/FOUNDER_CRITERIA.md`) are a flexible checklist. This idea scored 19/24; gaps are competition, proof of "hair on fire", and ERP integrations. Do not drop it only for missing a few.

## Biggest risks
Funded competitors (WizCommerce, Comena, Y Meadows); wrong-SKU errors (needs review step); reaching distributors; support load next to a 9-5.

## First moves when picked up
1. `community-discovery` for one vertical, then `research-outreach`.
2. 8-12 owner interviews (orders/day, minutes per order, error cost, ERP).
3. `demand-test`: 5 paid pilots at $400-500/mo.
4. Check employment contract and Indian export-tax setup with a CA.

Kill if: fewer than 6 of 10 owners key 20+ orders/day by hand, or fewer than 3 of 5 pilots pay by week 6.
