# Sector check: small and mid-size manufacturers
Date: 2026-10-24. Proposed by the founder: manufacturing lags in AI and software, so look for pains there. One round of web searches (vendor pages, trade press, a few surveys; mostly vendor-authored). 🔶 assumption; 🔵 open question.

## Verdict
**The sector is genuinely behind, but it does not beat the current plan.** Best manufacturing options score 15-17 of 24 against the 12 criteria (the jan-san order desk scored about 19). The most valuable wedge, quoting, already has funded startups.

## Is it lagging? Yes
- 73% of mid-market manufacturers are still testing AI and none reported full deployment (Kaufman Rossin, July 2026); barriers were legacy ERP and siloed data. [Source](https://www.marketscale.com/industries/industrial-iot/73-of-mid-market-manufacturers-are-still-testing-ai-with-zero-at-full-deployment.md)
- Census-based figures via MRPeasy show AI use among firms with 1-4 employees rising from about 10% to 19%, and 50-99 employees from about 14% to 30% (question wording changed in December 2025). The Federal Reserve estimates about 18% of all US firms use AI. [MRPeasy](https://www.mrpeasy.com/blog/?p=23199), [Fed note](https://www.federalreserve.gov/econres/notes/feds-notes/monitoring-ai-adoption-in-the-u-s-economy-20260403.html)
- Pains (vendor sources): 64% of MRPeasy customers used spreadsheets before adopting ERP; 52% cited admin burden and 37% disconnected tools; ERPs stop at work-order release while floor instructions stay on paper; document and drawing revision control is a constant problem. [MRPeasy](https://www.mrpeasy.com/blog/?p=22262), [AIS Corp](https://www.aiscorp.com/blog/what-manufacturers-are-actually-struggling-with)

## Why this does not beat the current plan
- Price anchors for tiny shops are $29-$250 a month, below the $400+ per customer needed for 30-50 clients; larger manufacturers pay more but need ERP integration and long evaluations.
- Quoting, the highest-value wedge, is already funded.
- The founder has no manufacturing knowledge or contacts.
