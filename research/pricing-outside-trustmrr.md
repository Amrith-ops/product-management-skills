# What do customers pay per month? A check outside TrustMRR
Date: 2026-10-24. Question: is TrustMRR overstating what businesses pay per customer? I searched the open web for typical prices in several domains.
**Evidence quality is low-to-medium:** these are search-result summaries of vendor and agency blogs, comparison sites and a few surveys. Almost none are independent, many disagree, and none are verified against primary data. Treat as ranges, not benchmarks.

## Is TrustMRR overhyping?
- **On price level: no, broadly consistent.** TrustMRR's top groups (SEO/AI-search, lead generation, outbound sales, IT help) charge $1,000-$3,000 a month per client; outside sources show the same bands for agency retainers ($500-$5,000), trades lead generation ($2,000-$6,000 including ad spend) and managed IT ($2,000-$3,000).
- **On "typical": yes, it overstates.** Outside surveys say most small and local clients pay under $1,000 a month for SEO, and 64% of agencies in one survey offer retainers under $1,000. The $3,000-plus figures are for bigger clients or sales-led firms. TrustMRR shows only businesses that chose to list, mostly in online marketing, and says nothing about acquisition cost or churn.
- **On software vs services:** the high per-customer prices outside are mostly **services with human expertise** (CFO, MSP, agencies) or **systems of record** (ServiceTitan, Procore, Dentrix). Standalone software tools for small businesses mostly sit at $40-$500 a month.

## Ranking by price per customer (outside evidence)
1. Large vertical platforms and enterprise software: $1k to $6k+ a month, but long sales cycles and big teams.
2. Fractional CFO: $2k-$15k a month (expert service).
3. Managed IT for small firms: $2k-$3k a month (service, on-call).
4. Trades marketing and lead generation: $2k-$6k a month including ads.
5. SEO / agency retainers: $500-$5k a month.
6. Compliance (SOC 2) platforms: about $600-$2,100 a month equivalent ($7.5k-$25k a year).
7. Systems of record for small shops: $170-$2,000 a month depending on size.
8. Bookkeeping: $300-$1,200 a month.
9. Practice software per seat: $40-$150 per user.

## What fits the founder's constraints (solo, India, 9-5, 30-50 clients or fewer)
- **Needs expertise or on-call:** fractional CFO, managed IT: poor fit (credentials, availability, liability).
- **Competes with systems of record:** poor fit for a solo founder.
- **Outcome-based growth services (lead generation, AI-search visibility) for one vertical:** price fits ($1k-$3k), but delivery effort and agency competition are real.
- **Compliance-adjacent tools:** buyers already budget $10k-$25k a year for compliance; that suggests the earlier Trustreply idea (security-questionnaire answers) may be underpriced at $149-$349 a month. 🔵 Test willingness to pay at $400-$800 a month.
- **Add-ons next to a system of record:** typical $100-$800 a month; needs many more clients than 30-50 unless priced as an outcome.
