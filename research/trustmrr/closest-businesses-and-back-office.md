## What this changes
1. **Done-for-you / managed services show up at the target scale:** about 20-75 clients at $1,000-$4,000 a month. That matches "30-50 clients" better than self-serve software does, but needs delivery effort next to a 9-5.
2. **Revenue-bringing offers lead:** lead generation for trades is the clearest analog to low-tech, few-client, high-price. This supports the earlier hypothesis to frame offers around money gained.
3. **Vertical depth pays:** Kibu-style focus on one vertical supports choosing one distributor vertical first, but note its compliance burden.
4. **Productize the service:** combine AI-assisted delivery with a fixed monthly price per client (for example, "order desk as a service" for distributors: the owner forwards orders, you return clean entries each day). This tests demand with a service before building software, and fits the parked idea.
