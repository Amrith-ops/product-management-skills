# Which business categories pay the most per month?
Date: 2026-10-24. Question from the founder after seeing AEO Engine charge up to $10,000 a month on TrustMRR.
Method: 724 TrustMRR profiles collected from category and channel pages (first 30 listings per page; the site's page parameter returns the same list, so this is a sample). I used businesses with MRR of at least $1,000 and at least 5 subscribers (348), and computed revenue per subscriber = MRR / active subscriptions. Revenue, MRR and subscriptions are payment-verified per TrustMRR; descriptions, categories and pricing text are founder-written. Businesses carry several category tags, so they count in each.

## Short answer
Per customer, the highest-paying group is **marketing, lead-generation and SEO/"AI search" visibility, mostly sold as done-for-you services to businesses that already spend on growth**. Next are sales tools, customer-support services, and managed IT help.

## Spotlight: AEO Engine (https://trustmrr.com/startup/aeo-engine)
- What it is: an "AI SEO agency" that gets brands cited in AI Overviews, ChatGPT and Google.
- Pricing (as written): Growth $1,597, Scale $2,997, then $5,000, $8,000 and $10,000 a month.
- Verified MRR $170.8k from 57 subscribers, about $2,997 each; audience B2B (SaaS, legal, services, ecommerce); founder-led; founded 2018 (an established agency, not a new startup).
- Monthly revenue grew from about $62k (Nov 2025) to about $165k (Sep and Oct 2026).
- It is a service business: revenue depends on delivery capacity, not software margin.

## Why these categories pay more
1. **The customer buys revenue, not savings.** Leads, rankings, citations in AI answers, outbound meetings. A client who earns $5k per new customer will pay $1.5k a month if it produces a few. (Interpretation; consistent with the table, not proven by it.)
2. **Buyers have budgets and measure ROI:** SaaS companies, agencies, ecommerce brands, trades and construction firms.
3. **Done-for-you service with AI leverage:** several top entries are agencies (AEO Engine, Indexsy, Windmill Growth, trades lead generation, a Reddit agency). Fewer clients at higher price, but delivery work.
4. **A new, fast-moving problem:** getting recommended by ChatGPT-style assistants ("AEO/GEO") shows up in four of the top entries. Performance-based pricing exists ("get recommended or you don't pay").
5. **Tiered ladders** with a top tier of 3 to 7 times the entry price.

## Caveats
- Revenue per subscriber is MRR divided by subscriptions; it can include add-ons or one-off payments. Few-subscriber entries (for example 7 subscribers) are noisy.
- Sample is biased toward online-marketing founders on TrustMRR; low-tech offline businesses are under-represented, so I cannot rank their categories.
- This shows what customers pay, not how hard it is to win them or retain them. Churn and acquisition cost are not public.
- Many top entries are labor-heavy services; that conflicts with a 9-5 unless the delivery is mostly automated.

## What this means for the founder
- **Price is the lever:** at $500 per client you need 32-40 clients for $16-20k; at $1,500 you need about 11-14. Categories with $1k+ per client are the ones selling revenue outcomes.
- **Reframe, don't abandon:** for the parked idea (order entry for distributors), test whether owners pay more for revenue outcomes (win-backs of dormant customers, faster quote follow-up, fewer lost orders) than for cost savings. 🔵
- **New candidate to score:** AI-search visibility ("AEO") for owner-operated businesses in one vertical. Rough fit with the 12 criteria: strong on USD, remote, price and buyer need; weaker on competition (several funded tools and agencies), delivery effort next to a 9-5, and claims about guaranteed results. Not yet scored with evidence.
