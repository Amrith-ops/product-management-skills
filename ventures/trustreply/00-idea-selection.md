# Step 0 - Idea selection

## Criteria
1. Painful and recurring (people already pay money or lose deals today).
2. Western B2B buyer with a budget (US/UK/EU), reachable without a sales team.
3. Solo founder can build an MVP in ~4-6 weeks with AI-assisted coding.
4. $12-15k MRR reachable with ~60-90 customers (price points of $149-$349/mo).
5. Defensible enough: workflow + trust, not just a prompt wrapper.

## Candidates considered
| Idea | Pain | Fit | Why not / why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Missed-call text-back for contractors | medium | crowded | many cheap clones |
| Failed-payment recovery for Stripe SaaS | high | crowded | Churnkey, Stripe built-ins |
| EU Accessibility Act scanner for small ecommerce | medium-high | deadline-driven | controversial incumbents, hard to prove value |
| Bookkeeper client-document chasing | high | niche | strong, but low willingness to pay per seat |
| **Security questionnaire autopilot for small SaaS** | **high** | **good** | **chosen** |

## Chosen problem
Small B2B SaaS companies (5-50 people) lose or delay deals because an enterprise buyer
sends a 100-400 question security questionnaire (SIG, CAIQ, custom spreadsheets). A founder,
CTO, or sales engineer spends 8-20 hours per questionnaire copy-pasting from policies and old
answers, and the deal stalls meanwhile. 🔶 Frequency: several per quarter once selling upmarket.

## Why it can reach the revenue target
40 x $149 + 20 x $349 = about $12.9k MRR from 60 customers. 🔶 Assumes $149/$349 tiers hold.

## Biggest risks (carried forward)
- Incumbents (compliance platforms) bundle this feature. 🔵
- Buyers will not upload sensitive policies to a new vendor. 🔵
- Accuracy: a wrong security answer is worse than a slow one. Mitigation: cite-or-abstain.
