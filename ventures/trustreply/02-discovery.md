# Step 2 - Discover
Skills: `/discover`, `discovery-interview-prep`, `voice-of-customer-miner`
**Status: plan only. No interviews have been run.**

## Goal
Learn whether the pain is frequent, costly, and unsolved enough that Frankie pays $149+/mo.

## Who to interview (target 12, minimum 8)
Founders/CTOs/sales engineers at 5-50 person B2B SaaS who received a security questionnaire in the last 6 months.
Sources: LinkedIn search, r/SaaS, Indie Hackers, founder Slack/Discord communities, fractional CISOs and SOC 2 auditors (as referrers).

## Screener
1. Do you sell B2B software? 2. Have you received a security questionnaire in the past 6 months? 3. Who answered it?

## Interview guide (past behavior only, no pitching)
1. Walk me through the last questionnaire you received. Start to finish.
2. How long did it take, and who was involved?
3. Where did the answers come from?
4. What happened to the deal during that time?
5. What did you try to make it faster? What did you pay for?
6. What would have to be true for you to trust a tool with this?
7. Who else would have to approve using it?

## Hypotheses and kill criteria
| # | Hypothesis | Kill if |
|---|---|---|
| H1 | Target teams get 3+ questionnaires per quarter | <40% of interviewees get 3+ |
| H2 | Each takes 8+ hours | median <4 hours |
| H3 | It delays or blocks deals | fewer than 1 in 3 report delay |
| H4 | They currently use spreadsheets/docs, not a purpose-built tool | most already pay for a tool and are happy |
| H5 | They would upload policies to a new vendor under reasonable safeguards | majority refuse outright |
| H6 | A $149/mo price is acceptable | most say it must be under $50 |

## Desk research (run in parallel)
Mine G2/Capterra/Reddit reviews of Vanta, Drata, Loopio, Conveyor for complaints about questionnaire handling and price (`voice-of-customer-miner`).

## Output when done
Table of interviews, quotes, hypothesis verdicts, go/no-go. 🔵 Everything above is still open.
