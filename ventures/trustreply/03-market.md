# Step 3 - Size and scan the market
Skills: `tam-sam-som-calculator`, `competitive-research-snapshot`, `market-landscape-scan`
**All figures are 🔶 from model knowledge, no live sources. Verify before relying on them.**

## Sizing (bottom-up)
| Layer | Assumption | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Companies | 🔶 ~80k B2B SaaS companies with 5-100 staff across US/UK/EU | 80,000 |
| TAM | x $2,000/yr average spend on this job | ~$160M |
| SAM | 🔶 ~30% sell to buyers that run security reviews regularly (24,000) x $2,000 | ~$48M |
| SOM (12-18 mo) | 60 customers x ~$2,150 avg ACV | ~$130k ARR = ~$12.9k MRR |

SOM is about 0.25% of the 24,000 SAM companies. The target needs few customers; the risk is reach, not market size.

## Competitive landscape
| Group | Examples (🔶 verify) | Typical buyer | Gap for small teams |
|---|---|---|---|
| Compliance automation | Vanta, Drata, Secureframe | Series A+ with audit goal | priced for a full compliance program; questionnaire AI is a side feature |
| RFP/response platforms | Loopio, Responsive | enterprise sales orgs | heavy, sales-led, expensive |
| Security-questionnaire specialists | Conveyor, Skypher, Vendict | mid-market | often demo-gated, higher price |
| Trust centers | SafeBase, Whistic | companies pre-empting questions | solves publishing, not answering |
| DIY | ChatGPT + spreadsheets | everyone small | no citation, no memory, no audit trail |

## Porter-style read
- Substitutes (DIY with a general LLM) are the strongest force; our edge is cited, abstaining, reusable answers.
- Supplier power: LLM providers; keep the model layer swappable.
- Threat of entrants: high. Moat must come from the answer library, trust, and workflow, not the prompt.

## Whitespace
Self-serve, card-swipe, under $400/mo, usable in one afternoon, for teams that do not yet need a compliance platform.
