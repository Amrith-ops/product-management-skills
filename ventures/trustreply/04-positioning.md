## Positioning statement
**For** founders and technical leads at small B2B SaaS companies
- **that need** to clear buyer security reviews without a security team
- Trustreply
- **is a** security-questionnaire assistant
- **that** drafts every answer from your own policies with a citation, and flags anything it cannot prove.

**Unlike** compliance suites and generic chatbots,
- Trustreply **provides** a first draft in minutes, never invents an answer, and builds a reusable answer library, from $149/mo with no sales call.
