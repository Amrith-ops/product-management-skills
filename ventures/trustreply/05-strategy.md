# Step 5 - Strategy
Skill: `/strategy`, `product-strategy-session`

## Where to play / how to win
- **Segment:** B2B SaaS, 5-50 staff, selling to mid-market and enterprise, pre-compliance-platform.
- **Win by:** speed to first value (one afternoon), cited answers (trust), low price, self-serve.
- **Not doing:** SOC 2 automation, trust centers, portal scraping, enterprise sales.

## Pricing hypothesis 🔶
- Starter $149/mo: up to 5 questionnaires/mo, 1 user.
- Team $349/mo: unlimited questionnaires, 5 users, answer library, audit export.
- Annual discount 2 months free. Free: 1 sample questionnaire to see the draft.

## Go-to-market (solo, no ads at first)
1. **Referral channels:** fractional CISOs, SOC 2 auditors, startup lawyers.
2. **Search:** pages for "SIG Lite answers", "CAIQ template", "how to answer a security questionnaire".
3. **Outbound:** founders posting about "security review" blockers; lists of Series Seed/A SaaS.
4. **Launch:** Product Hunt, Indie Hackers, relevant subreddits with a free sample tool.

## Strategic risks
1. Incumbents bundle the feature - respond by staying cheapest and simplest.
2. Data-trust barrier - invest early in a plain-English security page and DPA.
3. Accuracy failure - cite-or-abstain, human approval step, edit tracking.

## Success definition
60 paying customers at ~$215 average, churn under 4%/mo, by month 12. 🔶
