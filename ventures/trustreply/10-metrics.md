# Step 10 - Measure
Skills: `saas-revenue-growth-metrics`, `saas-economics-efficiency-metrics`, `acquisition-channel-advisor`

## North star
Questionnaires exported per paying account per month.

## Metrics
| Area | Metric | Target |
|---|---|---|
| Activation | signup -> first export within 7 days | 40% |
| Quality | unedited-accept rate | 70%+ |
| Quality | uncited claims shown as fact | 0 |
| Retention | monthly logo churn | under 4% |
| Revenue | MRR | $1.5k m3, $5k m6, $9k m9, $13k m12 |
| Economics | CAC payback | under 4 months |
| Economics | LTV:CAC | 3x+ |

## Channel scorecard (review monthly)
Referral (CISOs/auditors), SEO, outbound, community. Keep the one with the lowest CAC, test one new channel per month.

## Review cadence
Weekly: activation and accept rate. Monthly: MRR, churn, CAC by channel. Quarterly: re-run discovery with churned and active users, and `competitive-intel-watch`.

## Feedback into the loop
Low accept rate -> Step 7 probe again. Low activation -> Step 2 interviews. Low conversion -> Step 4 positioning.
